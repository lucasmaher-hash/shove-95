# Privacy Policy — shove.95

**Last updated:** 4 August 2026

## The short version

shove.95 collects nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking,
no account, and no server belonging to the developer. Your tasks and photos are
stored on your device, and — if you turn on iCloud sync — in your own private
iCloud account, which the developer cannot read.

## What the app stores

Everything you type or attach:

- task text
- the day a task is filed under
- whether it's done, flagged important, or archived
- photos you attach
- your settings: colour scheme, tab names, workspace names

This is held on your device. If you are signed into iCloud and sync is enabled,
it is also mirrored to your **private CloudKit database** — the part of iCloud
that belongs to your Apple Account. Apple provides this storage; the developer
has no access to its contents, cannot browse it, and receives no copy.

## What the app does not do

- No analytics or crash reporting SDKs
- No advertising or advertising identifiers
- No third-party services of any kind
- No account, login, or email address
- No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to the developer

## Photos

Photos you attach are downscaled and stored with the task. The app asks for
photo library access only when you tap the add-photo control, and for camera
access only if you choose the camera. It reads only the photos you pick. It
never scans your library.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a task deletes it and its photos. Deleting the app removes everything
stored on the device. If sync was on, removing the app's data from iCloud is
done in **Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage**.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address
