# Change plan — 2026-08-17

44 items from the founder, grouped by what they actually cost and ordered
easiest first. Worked in batches of ten; the founder checks between batches.

Three answers settled up front (2026-08-17):

- **Per-tab time toggle** — turning it off means the tab *moves nothing*. Tasks
  stay where they are: no rollover at midnight, no pull into Today, no overdue
  chip. The tab keeps its name and behaves like General does today.
- **Shared colour** — one setting, five slots, matched **by position**. Slot 1
  is Windows Standard / Cream, slot 2 Desert / Clay, and so on. Each look draws
  its own palette for the shared slot, so the two cannot drift.
- **Onboarding** — runs immediately on first launch, over the empty list, using
  a sample task that is removed afterwards.

Two items are analysis only and produce no code: the font-size table (#15) and
the honest assessment (#36). One is a note to self: the retro look and extra
colours may later sit behind an App Store rating (#18) — nothing built now, but
the colour and face settings should stay easy to gate.
